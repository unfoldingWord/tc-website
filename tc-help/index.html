translationCore 4 Help

Step-by-step help for Bible translators using translationCore 4 (tC4): draft beside the sources, understand the passage, check and align your work, and translate Open Bible Stories.

Shipped works in this build (60) · Partly built some parts missing (3) · Not yet planned, not in the app (3)

Pre-release software. tC4 is a pre-release (4.0.0-rc.1). Do not use it for production translation work yet. The newest pages describe build 79d3017 (main, Oct 5, 2026), which includes the v4.0.0-rc.1 release plus later fixes: export, import, Settings on the Home card, and more. See What's new in 4.0 rc.1 (Increment 8).

Getting started

Install tC4, find your way around Home, and learn the three modes.

Partly builtalpha.1Install and start tC4Download the build for your computer, install or unpack it, and start the app. It opens on the Home screen. Shippedalpha.4The Home screenHome lists your projects, most recently used first. From here you open a book or story, add a project with + Add a project, share a project to Door43, and open its Settings. Shippedalpha.4Understand, Translate and Check — the three modesEvery open project has three modes. Understand the passage, Translate it, then Check it — in any order. Shippedalpha.4Saving and checkpointstC4 saves as you work. The indicator at the top right tells you the truth about your last save, and offers Retry if a save failed. Shippedalpha.4Resume where you left offThe Resume banner on Home takes you straight back to the book, chapter and mode where you last made a change — Understand, Translate, or the checking tool you were using — even after you close and restart the app. Shippedalpha.4Switch project or go back HomeClick the project name at the top left to leave the project and return to Home, where you can open another project or book.

Bible projects

Create, import, open and export a Bible translation project.

Shippedalpha.1Create a Bible projectA Bible project is one translation in one language and one text direction. Name it, give the language, choose the direction, and add books next. Shippedalpha.1Add a bookAdd one book, or several at once, to a Bible project. The drafting sources (unfoldingWord ULT + UST v91) come with the app, and a new book opens in Understand. Shippedalpha.1Open a book and move between chaptersClick a book tile on Home to open it. Inside the project, the book list on the left moves you between books and chapters. Shippedrc.1Import a project (translationCore 3, USFM or Scripture Burrito)Bring existing work into tC4 as a new project — from translationCore 3 project zips, USFM files, or a Scripture Burrito zip. Nothing is created until you confirm. Shippedrc.1Export a book as PDFMake a print-ready, dated PDF of the open book, laid out with your Page setup choices. Shippedrc.1Export a book as USFM (aligned or plain)Save the open book as a USFM file — with your word alignments woven in, or plain text only. Shippedrc.1Export a project as a Scripture BurritoSave the whole project — text, alignments and checking decisions — as one Scripture Burrito zip. Works for Bible and Open Bible Stories projects.

Translate

Draft your translation beside the source texts, a section or a verse at a time.

Shippedalpha.1 (redesigned alpha.5)The Translate screenTranslate shows the source text on the left and your translation on the right, one row per section, with the book list and the helps panel beside them. Shippedalpha.1 (original-language pane alpha.5)Source tabs — gateway texts and the original languageSwitch the source column between the literal text, the simplified text and the Greek or Hebrew original. Shippedalpha.5Draft a sectionType a whole section as one piece of text, then place the verse numbers. This is the main way to draft in tC4. Shippedalpha.5Place verse numbersAfter typing a section, pick up each verse number and click the word where that verse begins. Shippedalpha.5Make or break a verse spanJoin two or more verses into one span, such as 2–3, when your language needs to reorder their content. Break the span again the same way. Bible projects only. Shippedalpha.1Draft or revise one verseSwitch to Verse to draft a single verse, or to fix one verse without opening the whole section. Shippedalpha.5Paragraphs and poetryStart a new paragraph with Enter and indent a poetry line with Tab while you type a section. Shippedalpha.4The helps panel in TranslateWhile you draft, the helps panel shows the notes, word links, questions, simplified text and your own comments for the verses in focus. Shippedalpha.2Editing a verse you already checked or alignedWhen you change a verse that has check decisions or alignments, tC4 flags them for review. Nothing is deleted.

Understand

Read a passage with its notes, word links and questions before you translate.

Shippedalpha.5Read a passage before you translateUnderstand shows the source passage section by section, with its helps beside it and a box for your own comments. Shippedalpha.5Translation notes and Translation Academy articlesTranslation notes explain hard phrases verse by verse. Each note can link to a Translation Academy article that teaches the translation issue behind it. Shippedalpha.5Word links (Translation Words)Word links point out the key biblical terms in the passage and link each to a Translation Words article that defines it. Shippedalpha.5Translation questionsTranslation questions ask what the passage says. Use them to test whether your translation communicates the meaning. Shippedalpha.5The simplified text tabThe helps panel can show the meaning-based simplified text of the chapter next to what you are reading. Shippedalpha.5Write a user commentWrite down what a passage means, who is speaking, and what will be hard to translate. Your comments are saved with the project and shown in Translate.

Check

Check your draft with Translation Notes and Translation Words, and preview it.

Shippedalpha.2Choose a checking toolThe Check screen lists the checking tools for the open book and how far you have got in each. Shippedalpha.2Check with Translation NotesWork through every note in the book that links to a Translation Academy article, and confirm how your translation handles each one. Shippedalpha.2Check with Translation WordsCheck every key term in the book against its definition, so important words are translated accurately and consistently. Shippedalpha.2Decide on a check item (valid, invalid, to do)For each check, select the words in your translation, then mark the item valid or invalid, or leave it to do. Shippedalpha.6Bookmark a checkFlag a check item to come back to it later, for example to discuss with your team. Shippedalpha.6Write a check commentAdd a note to a check item about what you decided or what to ask your team. Shippedalpha.2Filter and sort check itemsNarrow the list to what still needs work, and change the order of items. Shippedalpha.5Community Checking previewRead the whole book as a printed page, set up the page, and export a copy for reviewers.

Align

Link each word of your translation to the original language.

Shippedalpha.2Open the Align toolAlign links each word of your translation to the original-language word it translates. Open it from the Check screen. Shippedalpha.2Link your words to the sourceClick a word in the word bank and then the card it belongs under, or drag it there. Shippedalpha.2Align a phrase (merge and split cards)When several source words are translated together, merge their cards into one phrase card. SPLIT undoes it. Shippedalpha.4Align to the gateway text or the original languageAlign to the gateway words you can read (which already carry the link to the original), or directly to the Greek or Hebrew words. Shippedalpha.6Alignment suggestionstC4 can learn from the verses you have already aligned and suggest links for the next ones. You confirm or reject each one. Shippedalpha.2Mark an alignment doneWhen a verse is aligned, mark it valid. It counts toward the verses-aligned progress.

Open Bible Stories

Translate the fifty Open Bible Stories frame by frame.

Shippedalpha.7Start an Open Bible Stories projectCreate a project for translating all fifty Open Bible Stories into your language. Shippedalpha.7Find a story on HomeThe stories card shows your three most recent stories. Show all 50 to pick any story. Shippedalpha.7Translate a storyDraft the story title, each frame, and the reference line, one at a time, with the picture and the gateway story beside you. Shippedalpha.7Understand a storyRead each frame with its picture and the story helps, and write a comment for each frame. Shippedalpha.7Check a storyCheck a story with Translation Notes and Translation Words, frame by frame. Shippedalpha.7Story preview and exportPreview a story as a page with its pictures, adjust page setup, and export PDF, Story Markdown or a Scripture Burrito. Shippedrc.1Export stories as PDF or MarkdownSave a print-ready PDF of the stories, a Story Markdown zip, or both alongside Scripture Burrito — from the same Export menu as Bible.

Resources and offline

Source texts, helps, downloads, upgrades and working without a network.

Shippedalpha.2What comes with the apptC4 installs English source texts, helps and the Greek and Hebrew texts, so you can translate and check without internet. Shippedalpha.4Download source textsDownload source texts and helps for another gateway language from Door43. Shippedalpha.6Change the checking languageChoose which installed gateway language this project checks against. The choice is in Project settings. Decisions that match a check in the new notes carry over; the rest come back as work. Shippedalpha.6Update pinned helpsSee which resource versions your project uses, and move to newer releases when you are ready. Shippednew since alpha.7Update scripture textsMove the project to a newer release of the Greek or Hebrew text, or of the gateway Bible. Shippedalpha.4Fix a missing resourceWhen a checking tool needs a resource that is not on this computer, tC4 offers three ways to get it. Shippedalpha.2Working offlinetC4 works without internet. It only goes online when you ask it to, for downloads and updates.

Settings

Project settings and where tC4 keeps your work.

Partly builtalpha.4Project settingsOpen a project's settings from the Settings button on its Home card. Change the text direction, the script font, or the checking language, or manage its source texts. The name and language are shown but cannot be changed yet. Shippedalpha.4Where your projects are storedtC4 keeps your projects in one folder in your home folder. Back it up regularly. Not yetApp languageThe app's menus and buttons are in English only. Choosing another interface language is planned.

Troubleshooting and known limits

What the messages mean, and what is not built yet.

Shippedalpha.4When a save failsIf a save fails, your text is kept in the app. Use Retry, and do not close the app until it says Saved. Shippedalpha.4Messages that protect your projectWhen tC4 finds something unexpected, it stops rather than risk your work, and tells you what to do. Damaged import files are refused with a reason and a code, and nothing is created. Shippednew since alpha.7If the app closed unexpectedly or the window went blanktC4 keeps a log of each project operation and finishes or cleans up an interrupted step at the next start. If a screen cannot be drawn, tC4 shows a message with Back to Home, and Window › Reload brings the app back without quitting. Partly builtalpha.4Windows, macOS and Linux notesThe alpha builds are unsigned. Here is what to expect on each system, and the known problems. Shippednew since alpha.7Share to Door43Sign in to Door43 once, choose your account or an organization, and push the project's working main branch. Later shares upload changes again. Not yetWorking as a team on several computerstC4 keeps each project on one computer. Syncing a project between computers is not built yet. Not yetFeatures not built yetA list of things people often ask for that this build does not do yet.
