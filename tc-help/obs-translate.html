Translate a story

Shippedalpha.7

Draft the story title, each frame, and the reference line, one at a time, with the picture and the gateway story beside you.

Where in the app: Open a story › Translate

What it is

The story Translate screen is like Bible drafting, but the units are the title, the frames and the reference.

When to use it

To draft or edit a story.

Step by step

  1. Open a story from Home (or pick one in the Stories rail; Toggle story list shows or hides it).
  2. Click Draft the title and type the story title.
  3. Click Save, or just click outside the box, to save.
  4. For each frame, click "Draft frame N", read the picture and gateway text, and type your translation.
  5. Click Draft the reference and translate the reference line (for example "A Bible story from: Genesis 1-2").
  6. To change a saved part, click it (Edit this text).

What you should see

  • The rail lists Stories. The main area shows Story title, each "Frame N" with its picture and Gateway story text, and Reference.
  • Empty parts show buttons: Draft the title, "Draft frame N" and Draft the reference.
  • While editing: a box with the hint Type your translation…, and Save / Cancel. The source badge reads like "Gateway story · v9 · pinned".

Screenshots

Tips

  • The frame numbers in the rail show which frames are drafted.
  • Use the helps panel (Toggle helps panel) for notes and word links while drafting.
  • With no frame open for editing, the Stories rail still listed plain names ("Story 1", "Story 2", and so on) and did not show percentages. Frame 2 was highlighted in the rail while the page was scrolled to frame 16.
  • Known issue: While a frame is open for editing, the rail has shown the same plain names. A story's percent drafted may not update right away.
  • Known issue: With no comments on the story, the Comments tab says "Nothing in this chapter." (Bible wording). It means the story has no comments yet (see screenshot 4).

Note: Verse spans and verse numbers do not exist in stories. They are only for Bible books.

Known limits

  • If the gateway story is missing, the screen says "Gateway text is unavailable." with Get source.
  • If no picture matches, the screen lists the image lines it could not find.
