Understand a story

Shippedalpha.7

Read each frame with its picture and the story helps, and write a comment for each frame.

Where in the app: Open a story › Understand

What it is

The same idea as Read a passage before you translate, for stories. Comments are written per frame.

Step by step

  1. Open a story and click Understand.
  2. Read the title and each frame with its picture.
  3. Click a frame to see its notes, word links and questions in the helps panel. Until you click a frame, the panel may show the helps for another frame.
  4. Click in the frame's comment box, type your comment, then click outside to save.

What you should see

  • The screen says "Read the story and its helps before you translate."
  • Each frame shows its picture, the gateway story text and a comment box.
  • The helps panel has Notes, Words and Questions for the selected frame.

Screenshots

Tips

  • The comment box under the story title reads "What does this frame say, and what will be hard to render?" Nothing was typed in it.
  • In the helps panel, story notes are labelled like verses. On frame 1 each card said NOTE v1. The first note quoted "the beginning", then "created", "the universe", and "the earth". The notes were in English.
  • Known issue: Your first click in a comment box selects the frame and can scroll the page, and only part of what you type may be saved. Click the box again once the page stops moving, type, and check that the whole comment was kept.
  • If a comment fails to save, the app says "The user comment was not saved — edit the box and leave it again to retry."

Known limits

  • There is no simplified-text tab for stories.
  • Saved comments cannot be emptied, as for Bible projects.
