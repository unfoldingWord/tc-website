Check with Translation Notes

Shippedalpha.2

Work through every note in the book that links to a Translation Academy article, and confirm how your translation handles each one.

Where in the app: Check › Translation Notes card

What it is

The Translation Notes tool builds a checklist from the notes that point to a Translation Academy article, such as figures of speech, implied information or passive voice. For each one you find the words in your translation that render the quoted phrase and decide whether they are right.

When to use it

After drafting, to check hard translation issues one by one.

Step by step

  1. Click Check, then the Translation Notes card. If the last checking tool you used opens instead of the list, click ← All checking tools.
  2. Read What to check. The quoted phrase is highlighted in the gateway pane. On the Spanish checking language that pane was GATEWAY · GLT, and the quote was "conforme a la fe". It is not always the English ULT.
  3. Click "Learn more: … →" if you want the full lesson.
  4. Under Your translation, tap the word or words that render the quote.
  5. Decide: see Decide on a check item.
  6. Marking an item does not move you on. Click the → arrow at the top right of the item (above the quote) to go to the next item.

What you should see

  • The item shows What to check with the note, the original-language and gateway texts (the quote is highlighted in the gateway text), and Your translation with the verse.
  • The rail on the left lists items, with filters and two Sort buttons, By verse and By category.
  • Learn more: {title} → opens the Translation Academy article in a drawer.

Tips

  • On Sample English the tool opens at Item 2 of 157, Titus 1:1, quote "of the chosen people of God". Progress is 1 of 157 resolved, and To do is 156. The first rail row, "for the faith of the chosen people…", has a green dot and is not the open row. Sort is By verse. The resource line is git.door43.org/unfoldingWord/en_tn · v91 · primary gateway language. Learn more says Active or Passive. The gateway pane is Gateway · unfoldingWord Literal Text, and it highlights the quote. Your translation reads "Paul a servant of God", the same missing comma and period as in Translation Words. Nothing was marked.
  • The notes come with the app: a new project checks against the English notes v91 (en_tn), with no download.
  • A Bible project and a stories project each use their own notes. After you check a story, Titus still opens on the Titus notes (fixed in Increment 8, issue #434).
  • By category groups items by translation issue (all metaphors together, for example). This is useful to learn one issue at a time.
  • If the draft changes after you decided an item, tC4 marks the decision for re-review: "{n} decision(s) no longer match the draft text and need re-review. Nothing was discarded."

Known limits

  • Only notes with a Translation Academy link become check items. Other notes stay in the helps panel.
