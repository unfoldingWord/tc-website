Help › Understand

Read a passage before you translate

Shippedalpha.5

Understand shows the source passage section by section, with its helps beside it and a box for your own comments.

Where in the app: Top bar › Understand

What it is

Understand is a reading screen. It shows the gateway source text of the chapter split into sections (or single verses), and the translation helps for the part you select. Many teams read and discuss a passage here before drafting it.

When to use it

Before you draft a passage, and whenever the team discusses meaning.

Step by step

  1. Open a book and click Understand in the top bar.
  2. Pick the chapter in the book list on the left (Toggle book list shows or hides it).
  3. Choose the source with the ULT or UST tab. A Spanish gateway uses GLT and GST instead.
  4. Choose Section or Verse next to Comment by to read by sections or by single verses. In Verse view, Titus 1:1 is its own block, and the empty comment box says "What does this verse mean, and what will be hard to render?"
  5. Click a block to select it. The helps panel on the right shows the notes, word links and questions for that block.
  6. Point at or click a help card to highlight the words it is about.

What you should see

  • The title reads the book and chapter with the line "Read the passage and its helps before you translate."
  • The source tabs sit above the passage. On this Spanish project they are GLT and GST. An English gateway shows ULT and UST. The Comment by Section / Verse switch is there too.
  • Each section is a block with its reference, the source text and a comment box. On Sample Titus the first block is "Titus 1:1–16". On Sample English the first block is "Titus 1:1–3", because the English literal text breaks the chapter there. The helps panel on the right shows the helps for the selected block.

Screenshots

Tips

  • A small green dot on a block's header means you have written a comment there.
  • The helps panel has Notes, Words, Questions and the simplified text tab. See the other pages in this section.
  • Your section/verse choice is shared with Translate.
  • Section breaks follow the gateway text. The same chapter can be one block in one language and several blocks in another. English Titus 1 starts as Titus 1:1–3. Spanish Titus 1 starts as Titus 1:1–16.
  • Under the ULT tab, Understand names the text "unfoldingWord Literal Text". It does not show the pinned version. Translate does.

Known limits

  • Understand shows the gateway texts (ULT, UST). The Greek or Hebrew original is shown in Translate and Check.
  • If the project uses different verse numbering from the source, Understand shows single verses only, and some verses may be noted as having "no place in the source's verse numbering" (issues #118, #121).
