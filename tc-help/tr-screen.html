The Translate screen

Shippedalpha.1 (redesigned alpha.5)

Translate shows the source text on the left and your translation on the right, one row per section, with the book list and the helps panel beside them.

Where in the app: Top bar › Translate

What it is

Translate is where you draft. The screen has four parts:

  1. The book list (left) — books and chapter numbers. Hide or show it with Toggle book list.
  2. The source column — the text you translate from. Tabs switch between the literal text (ULT), the simplified text (UST) and the original language (Greek for the New Testament, Hebrew for the Old). Under the tabs is the source's full name and pinned version, for example "unfoldingWord Literal Text · v91 · pinned".
  3. Your translation — one row per section, beside the matching source text. At the top right is the Section / Verse switch that chooses how you draft.
  4. The helps panel (right) — notes, word links, questions, the simplified text and your comments for the verses you are working on. Hide or show it with Toggle helps panel.

When to use it

Whenever you draft or revise your translation.

Step by step

  1. Open a book from Home (or click Translate in the top bar).
  2. Choose a chapter in the book list on the left.
  3. Read the source text in the left column. Switch tabs to compare ULT, UST and the original language.
  4. Choose Section to draft a whole section at once, or Verse to draft one verse at a time.
  5. Start drafting: see Draft a section or Draft or revise one verse.

On Offline371, Titus 1 is one section, verses 1–3. Verse 1 is already drafted ("Paul, a servant of God.") and it shows in the section row, with the numbers 2 and 3 after it. The section is not finished, so Draft section 1–3 is still there.

What you should see

  • The page title reads the book and chapter, for example "Titus 1".
  • The left column has source tabs (ULT, UST, Greek or Hebrew) and the source text; the right column is headed with your project name and language code.
  • Each row is one section of the chapter; an undrafted section shows a Draft section {span} button.

Screenshots

Tips

  • Sections follow the source text's section breaks, so your rows line up with the source.
  • tC4 remembers your Section / Verse choice for each project.

Known limits

  • ULT and UST cannot be shown side by side yet; use the tabs or the helps panel's simplified tab (issue #353).
  • The helps panel cannot be widened yet (issue #234).
  • If a project uses verse numbering that differs from the source, Translate works verse by verse only ("Verse view. This project's verse numbering differs from the source, so sections are not available.").
