Bookmark a check

Shippedalpha.6

Flag a check item to come back to it later, for example to discuss with your team.

Where in the app: Inside a checking tool › ⚑ Bookmark

What it is

A bookmark marks a check so you can find it again. It does not change the decision.

When to use it

When you are unsure and want to come back, or want to raise an item at a team meeting.

Step by step

  1. In a checking tool, open the item.
  2. Click ⚑ Bookmark. It now reads Bookmarked.
  3. Later, choose the Bookmarked filter in the rail to see all bookmarked items.
  4. To remove it, click Bookmarked again.

What you should see

  • The button changes from Bookmark to Bookmarked. The rail shows Pablo under PAUL with an orange flag and 1:1.
  • The Bookmarked filter lists only bookmarked items.

Tips
