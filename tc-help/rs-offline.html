Working offline

Shippedalpha.2

tC4 works without internet. It only goes online when you ask it to, for downloads and updates.

Where in the app: Everywhere. Internet is used only when you choose Go online.

What it is

Everything you need to translate and check English-gateway projects is installed with the app, and your projects are stored on your computer. The app starts with internet access switched off in the app. It goes online only when you click Go online.

What needs internet

Needs internetWorks offline
Download source textsCreate, open and resume projects
Update pinned helpsTranslate, Understand, Check, Align
Update scripture textsCommunity Checking and export
Option 1 in Fix a missing resourceImport

Step by step

  1. Work normally. No internet is needed.
  2. When you need a download, open Source texts and click Go online.

What you should see

  • When the app is offline, Source texts shows "The app is offline, so it cannot read the Door43 catalogue." and Go online.
  • Drafting, checking, aligning, exporting and importing all work offline.

Screenshots

Tips

  • A tool that needs a missing resource says Unavailable offline but never blocks the rest of your work.
  • After you restart tC4 it is offline again. Click Go online when you need the network, for example to download or check for updates.

Offline: Keep regular backups of the projects folder (see Where your projects are stored); there is no online sync yet.

Known limits

  • Sharing to Door43 and team sync are not built yet.
