Filter and sort check items

Shippedalpha.2

Narrow the list to what still needs work, and change the order of items.

Where in the app: Inside a checking tool › the rail › filter chips and Sort

What it is

The rail on the left of a checking tool lists the items. You can filter and sort it.

FilterShows
Allevery item
To doundecided items
Invaliditems marked invalid
Bookmarkeditems with ⚑
Commenteditems with ✎
Sort buttonsTool
By word / By verseTranslation Words (Bible)
By verse / By categoryTranslation Notes (Bible)
By word / By frameTranslation Words (stories)
By frame / By categoryTranslation Notes (stories)

Step by step

  1. Open a checking tool.
  2. Click a filter chip in the rail.
  3. Next to Sort, click one of the two buttons to change the order.
  4. Click an item to open it.

What you should see

  • The filter chips read All · 223, To do · 223, Invalid · 0, Bookmarked · 0, Commented · 0. Invalid is filled.
  • Under the filters, Sort is followed by two buttons; the selected one is filled.
  • If nothing matches, the rail says "Nothing matches this filter."

Screenshots

Tips

  • Finish a pass with To do, then fix problems with Invalid.
  • When nothing matches, the rail says "Nothing matches this filter." The item that was already open can stay in the main pane.

Known limits

  • None known.
