Editing a verse you already checked or aligned

Shippedalpha.2

When you change a verse that has check decisions or alignments, tC4 flags them for review. Nothing is deleted.

Where in the app: Translate (the edit), then Check (the flags)

What it is

Your checks and alignments are tied to the exact words of a verse. When you change those words, tC4 keeps every decision and alignment but marks the ones that may no longer fit, so you can look at them again.

  • A check decision whose words changed becomes Invalid.
  • An alignment keeps the links the edit did not touch. Only the changed words come loose and go back to the word bank, and the verse's valid mark is taken back.

When to use it

Nothing to switch on — it happens by itself. Visit Check after you revise checked verses.

Step by step

  1. Check a verse (for example mark a Translation Notes item ✓ Mark valid) and align it.
  2. Go to Translate and change a word in that verse.
  3. Go back to Check and open the tool. Read the notice at the top.
  4. Click the Invalid filter to see the items that need review, and decide them again.
  5. Open Align for the verse, place the loose words from the word bank, and click ✓ Mark valid again.

What you should see

  • In a checking tool, a notice says "N decision(s) no longer match the draft text and need re-review. Nothing was discarded."
  • The affected items appear under the Invalid filter.
  • In Align, the changed word is back in the word bank (for example "25 of 26 words placed") and the verse shows as to do again in the rail.

Screenshots

Tips

  • Making or breaking a verse span also marks the checks and alignments of those verses for review.
  • A checking tool may reopen at the item you were on, not the first one that needs review. Use the Invalid filter to find them all.
  • Align shows no "The draft changed since this alignment was made" notice in this case, and the verse shows as to do (grey), not invalid: tC4 kept the links, so only the loose words need placing. Look for words back in the word bank. The notice appears when tC4 could not keep the links.
