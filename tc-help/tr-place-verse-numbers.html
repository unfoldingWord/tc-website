Place verse numbers

Shippedalpha.5

After typing a section, pick up each verse number and click the word where that verse begins.

Where in the app: Translate › section editor › Place verse numbers

What it is

In Place verse numbers mode the section's words are shown one by one and each verse number is a small marker. Numbers not placed yet wait in the To place bank. You move a number by picking it up and clicking the word its verse begins with.

When to use it

After you type a section in Type mode, or when a verse boundary is in the wrong place.

Step by step

  1. In the section editor, click Place verse numbers.
  2. Click a number in the To place bank to pick it up.
  3. Point at words: a ghost marker shows where the number would land, and the tooltip explains, for example "Begin verse 2 at Paul".
  4. Click the word where that verse begins.
  5. Repeat for every number, then click Save section.

What you should see

  • Every word shows separately; unplaced numbers wait in the To place bank.
  • The hint reads "Pick a number up, then click the word it begins at." and, while a number is held, "Click the word where verse N begins".
  • When all numbers are placed: "Every verse is placed. Pick a number up to move where its verse begins."

Screenshots

Tips

  • To move a number already placed, pick it up from the text and click another word.
  • A number cannot pass another number ("Verse N cannot pass another verse number").
  • The first number of the section is fixed ("Verse N begins the section and is fixed").
  • Dropping a number onto a word where another verse already begins joins the two into a verse span. See Make or break a verse span.
  • Switch back to Type at any time to fix words; the numbers you placed are kept.
  • Known issue: When you pick up a number that was already placed, the bank can still say "Every verse is placed…" next to "Click the word where verse N begins". Follow the "Click the word…" hint.
