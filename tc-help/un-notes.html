Translation notes and Translation Academy articles

Shippedalpha.5

Translation notes explain hard phrases verse by verse. Each note can link to a Translation Academy article that teaches the translation issue behind it.

Where in the app: Understand or Translate › helps panel › Notes › Translation Academy →

What it is

Translation Notes (tN) are short explanations of difficult words and phrases in the source text. Many notes point to a Translation Academy (tA) article, a short lesson about a translation issue such as metaphors or implied information.

When to use it

When a phrase is hard to understand or hard to express in your language.

Step by step

  1. In Understand, click a section (or verse) to select it.
  2. In the helps panel, click Notes.
  3. Read the note cards. Point at one to highlight its phrase in the passage.
  4. Click Translation Academy → on a note to open its article.
  5. Close the article with ✕.

What you should see

  • A note card shows a label such as vintro or a verse number, the phrase it is about, and the note text. Long notes have Show more.
  • Translation Academy → opens the article inside the panel. This one is Sustantivos abstractos, with a ✕ to close it.

Screenshots

Tips

  • Notes follow the project's checking language. On Sample Titus they are Spanish (Latin American), and they work offline once that package is installed.
  • On Sample English the notes are English. The first card is labeled vintro, "Titus 1 Chapter Introduction", and it has Show more. The next card is v1 and quotes "for the faith of the chosen people of God and knowledge of the truth". Opening Translation Academy on that note highlights the phrase and opens "Abstract Nouns". ✕ closes the article.
  • If you see "Shown from the English fallback", the checking-language notes are not on this computer and the English notes are shown instead.

Known limits

  • If an article is missing from the installed resources, the panel says "The pinned resources do not carry the article for this check." The note itself still shows.
  • The English chapter introduction shows a raw link in the note text: "rc://ta/man/translate/figs-abstractnouns". It is visible as written. It is not a button.
