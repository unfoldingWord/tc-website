What's new in 4.1

Helps cards grouped by verse with named Translation Academy links and word intros, Clear alignments on Align, story frame conflicts on sync, project-format sync guards, and an app language that follows the computer until you choose.

4.1.0-preview builds on the 4.0 rc.2 public beta. These are the main changes translators see.

Helps

  • Notes are grouped under verse headings. Cards sit under uppercase headings such as INTRODUCTION and VERSE 1. The old NOTE / KEY WORD badges and verse pills are gone.
  • Article links use the real name. A note that opens Translation Academy now says Abstract Nouns → (or the article's own title), not a generic Translation Academy →.
  • Word cards show the first paragraph of the Translation Words article, then Read the article →.
  • Click a word card to highlight every occurrence of that word across the section.
  • Articles render cleanly — line breaks, superscripts and underlines from the source no longer show as raw tags. A floating close control sits above a divider.

See The helps panel in Translate, Translation notes and Word links.

Align

  • Clear alignments sits on the same footer row as ✓ Mark valid. It asks first, then sends every placed word on that verse back to the word bank. Cards keep their original words.

See Mark an alignment done.

Team sync and stories

  • Story frame conflicts. When two teammates both change a frame, sync reports 1 frame conflict to review, and Home and Translate show an amber 1 conflict · Review chip.
  • Newer project format. If a teammate's newer translationCore saved the project in a format this version cannot save to, sync stops and names their format.
  • Repaired Door43 files. If format.json was deleted or damaged on Door43, tC4 puts this computer's copy back and tells you.

See Working as a team on several computers and Find a story on Home.

Language

  • App language follows the computer until you choose one in App language. After you pick, tC4 keeps your choice.
  • Change checking language no longer offers the language already in use, and the confirm actions sit side by side.

See Change the app language and Change the checking language.
